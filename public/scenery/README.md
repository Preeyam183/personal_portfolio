# Underwater scenery

`underwater-village.webp` was generated with the built-in ImageGen tool and converted to WebP for the website.

Prompt: Original hand-painted 2D cartoon underwater environment, saturated sky blue and light cyan water, beige sand, irregular inked outlines, watercolor/gouache texture, water caustics, sunbeams, distant flower motifs, coral and seaweed, whimsical original shell/metal/driftwood village houses at the lower corners, spacious central water for text. No text, logos, existing characters, pineapple houses, or exact landmarks.

## Continuous water update

- `open-water.webp`: built-in ImageGen edit of the village scene. Prompt: preserve the painted cyan water, surface ripples and sunbeams; remove houses, sand, coral, plants, fish and jellyfish; retain faint distant flower motifs; create open luminous blue water for a continuous page background.
- `painted-jellyfish.webp` (original PNG also retained): built-in ImageGen transparent asset. Prompt: a single warm pink jellyfish with purple spots, scalloped rim, five long wavy tentacles, gouache texture and imperfect magenta ink outlines matching the painted environment; no face, scenery, or text.

The requested Patrick/Squidward/SpongeBob house asset was rejected by the image tool’s output safety filter. The existing original village remains the animated fallback; no house sprite asset was produced.
